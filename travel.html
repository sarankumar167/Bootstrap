Khajjiar

Khajjiar, a picturesque hill station in Himachal Pradesh, is famously known as the "Mini Switzerland of India" due to its striking resemblance to the Swiss alpine landscape

Chitrakote

Chitrakote Falls, located in the Bastar district of Chhattisgarh, is widely celebrated as the "Niagara Falls of India"

Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep is India's smallest Union Territory, located in the Arabian Sea, featuring a breathtaking archipelago of 36 coral islands and atolls.
