Customer Support FAQs

How can i contact customer support?
You can contact our support team via email, phone, or live chat. Our representatives are available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
How long does it take to receive a response?
We aim to respond to all customer inquiries within 24 hours. Response times may vary during weekends and holidays.
What should I do if I receive a damaged product?
If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact our support team immediately with your order details and photos of the product. We will arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.
